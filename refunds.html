Refund policy

What happens to your money when plans change, for games you pay for through Bounce Up.

Last updated 21 October 2026

TermsPrivacyRefunds

In shortThe plain-English version

  • Organiser cancels the game: you get everything back, including the booking fee.
  • You leave more than 24 hours before the start: you get the game price back.
  • You leave within 24 hours of the start: no refund, because your spot is hard to fill.
  • Refunds are automatic and go back to the card you paid with.

02

How refunds work

  • Refunds happen automatically in the app. You don’t need to ask.
  • Money goes back to the card or wallet you paid with. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • The app tells you before you leave a paid game whether you’ll get money back.
  • The game price comes back from the organiser, and the booking fee from Bounce Up.

03

Waitlists on paid games

You’re never charged for joining a waitlist. When a spot opens in a paid game, everyone on the waitlist gets a notification and the first person to pay gets the spot.

04

Paying at the court

If you pay the organiser directly (cash, bank transfer or another app), the organiser’s own refund rules apply. Please sort it out with them.

05

If something goes wrong

If a game didn’t go ahead, wasn’t as described, or you think a refund is wrong, talk to the organiser first. If that doesn’t fix it, email benjibajout@gmail.com with the game name and date and we’ll help.

Our services come with guarantees that can’t be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. If there’s a major failure, you’re entitled to a refund, and nothing in this policy limits those rights.
