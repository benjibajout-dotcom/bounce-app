About Bounce Up

By creating an account or using Bounce Up you agree to these terms, our privacy policy and our refund policy. If you don’t agree, please don’t use Bounce Up.

02

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to create a Bounce Up account.
  • Give accurate details, keep your sign-in email secure, and use one account per person.
  • You’re responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • You can stop using Bounce Up at any time. To delete your account, email us and we’ll remove it.

03

Games, organisers and clubs

Games on Bounce Up are run by organisers, clubs, coaches and venues (“organisers”), not by Bounce Up. Organisers are responsible for their sessions, including:

  • accurate game details (time, place, level, price and what’s included)
  • booking courts or venues, and any permits they need
  • running the session safely, and having the insurance they need
  • following our refund rules when they take payment through Bounce Up
  • their own tax and GST obligations

Paid sessions and coaching need an organiser check: organisers send their ABN, public liability insurance and venue booking or permit, and coaches also send their coaching accreditation and Working with Children Check. We look at these papers before giving a Checked tick, but the organiser stays responsible for their sessions. Games where the price only covers costs, like court hire, don’t need a check.

Club committees act for their club. A committee member who sets up club payouts confirms they’re allowed to do that for the club.

04

Playing safely

Sport involves physical activity and a risk of injury. You take part at your own risk, to the extent the law allows. Check you’re fit to play, warm up, use the right gear, and follow the organiser’s and venue’s instructions.

Bounce Up doesn’t check players, organisers, coaches or venues. Ratings such as DUPR are entered by players themselves, and coaches are responsible for their own accreditation and any Working with Children Check they need. A club’s ABN is checked against the Australian Business Register when we can, but that only confirms the ABN, not the club’s activities.

Meet in public places, trust your instincts, and tell us about anyone who makes you feel unsafe. If someone is in danger, call 000.

05

Payments

Organisers can ask players to pay through Bounce Up when they join. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and Bounce Up never sees them.

  • Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST where it applies.
  • Players pay the organiser’s game price plus a A$0.99 booking fee for each paid spot. The booking fee is Bounce Up’s fee for the booking service.
  • The organiser receives the full game price, paid to their bank account by Stripe.
  • To receive payments, organisers and clubs set up a Stripe account and agree to the Stripe Connected Account Agreement.
  • Paying at the court, outside Bounce Up, is between you and the organiser.

Refunds follow our refund policy.

06

How to behave on Bounce Up

Be respectful on and off the court. Don’t:

  • harass, threaten, bully or discriminate against anyone
  • post anything unlawful, sexual, hateful, misleading or that you don’t have the right to share
  • post fake games, spam, or ask players to pay outside Bounce Up for a game listed as paid in Bounce Up
  • pretend to be someone else, or misuse other people’s details
  • copy, scrape or interfere with Bounce Up or other people’s accounts

We can remove content, cancel games or suspend accounts that break these terms or put people at risk. Where it’s fair to, we’ll tell you why.

07

What you post

You own the photos, posts and messages you put on Bounce Up. You give us permission to store and show them in Bounce Up so the service works (for example, showing your club post to its members). You can delete your posts at any time.

Game chats are seen by the people in that game, and club chats by the club’s members. Your profile name, photo, suburb, sports and level are visible to other Bounce Up users.
