Privacy policy

How Bounce Up collects, uses and looks after your personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles.

Last updated 23 October 2026

In shortThe plain-English version

  • We collect what Bounce Up needs to work: your account, profile, the games and clubs you join, and payment records.
  • Other players can see your public profile and the games you’re in. Chats stay with the people in them.

01

Who we are

Bounce Up is run by Benjamin Gerard, trading as Bounce Up (ABN 83 386 303 756), Victoria, Australia. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. For any privacy question, email benjibajout@gmail.com.

02

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your name and photo if you sign in with Google or Apple or add them yourself.
  • Age check: we ask your date of birth to check you’re 18 or over. We keep only a note that you confirmed it, not the date itself.
  • Safety: the people you block (so we can hide their games, posts and messages from you), and any reports you send or that are made about you, so we can deal with them.
  • Profile: display name, photo, suburb, sports, level, bio, and DUPR rating and ID if you add them.
  • Activity: games you host, join or wait for; clubs, posts, comments, likes and chat messages; lesson requests; league entries and scores.
  • Locations you choose to share: if you host a game and tap “drop a pin here”, your device’s location at that moment is saved as the game’s location and shown with the game. We don’t track your location otherwise.
  • Payments: what you paid, for which game, refunds, and Stripe reference numbers. Card details are collected by Stripe, not us.
  • Organisers and clubs: Stripe collects identity and bank details to pay you. We keep your Stripe account ID and whether payouts are on. A club’s ABN and its public details from the Australian Business Register.
  • Organiser checks: if you ask to run paid sessions or coach, your ABN and its details from the Australian Business Register, your public liability insurance, your venue booking or permit, your coaching accreditation and your Working with Children Check number, surname and expiry, plus the files you upload. Only you and the Bounce Up team can see them. Other people only see a Checked tick. You can delete them at any time.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the address your browser gives us to send them.
  • Technical: basic device and browser details and IP addresses in server logs, used to keep Bounce Up secure and working.

03

How we use it

  • to run Bounce Up: show games, fill spots, manage waitlists, clubs, chats, leagues and lessons
  • to take payments, pay organisers and process refunds
  • to send the notifications and emails you’ve asked for (sign-in links, reminders, waitlist spots)
  • to keep people safe, deal with reports and stop misuse
  • to understand how Bounce Up is used, so we can improve it
  • to meet our legal obligations, such as tax records

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising tracking.

04

What other people can see

  • Your profile (name, photo, suburb, sports, level, DUPR) is visible to other Bounce Up users.
  • Player lists show who’s going to a game. Club pages show the committee and members.
  • Game chats are visible to that game’s players and host. Club chats and posts are visible to club members.
  • Games, clubs, venues, leagues and coach profiles are public, including a club’s ABN and registered name.

05

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run Bounce Up. They only get what they need:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and servers (hosted in Singapore)
  • Cloudflare: hosts the Bounce Up website
  • Stripe: payments and organiser payouts (see Stripe’s privacy policy)
  • Google and Apple: if you sign in with them; Google Maps when you open a map
  • Apple, Google and Mozilla push services: to deliver notifications to your device
  • Australian Business Register: to check a club’s ABN

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in Singapore and the United States. We choose providers with strong security and privacy practices. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

06

Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted in transit, and database rules make sure people can only see and change what they’re allowed to. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we’ll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as the law requires.

07

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your information, except payment records, which we keep for at least five years for tax purposes, and anything we need to keep for safety or legal reasons.

08

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Turn notifications on or off in your profile.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account: email benjibajout@gmail.com. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Bounce Up is for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from children. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete their information.

09

Complaints and changes

If you’re worried about how we’ve handled your information, email benjibajout@gmail.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

We may update this policy. If a change is important, we’ll tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
